01 — About

Bio

I'm Akhylle Rei Resurreccion Toledo, a 20-year-old aspiring software engineer. My interest in tech traces back to elementary school, when I spent hours writing JASS scripts in the Warcraft III World Editor — tinkering with unit behaviors, abilities, and terrain just to build a game that matched what was in my head. My brother noticed how much time I sank into it, and that moment nudged me away from a near-detour into criminology and onto a tech path instead. Outside of code, I'm usually watching whatever media I'm into, cracking jokes with people I like, socializing, or tweaking things — games, tools, setups — until they fit exactly how I want them.
